# seir-x0-s0

This theory is the seasonally forced SEIR model of seir-core, fitted to each jurisdiction's admissions as seen through lens-admissions. It denies the covariate effect: the covariate is ignored, as if transmission did not depend on it. It denies the school effect: school closures, scheduled or imposed, leave transmission unchanged. The strengths are fixed at these values rather than fitted, so the theory makes a definite claim that its forecasts can confirm or refute.

The four seir theories share the same core, lens and fitting procedure and differ only in these two stances, so any two that differ in one stance form a matched pair: their difference in predictive score speaks to that mechanism alone, under the assumptions they share. Because the theory models transmission, it can also forecast intervention arms: a school closure acts through the school effect and a vaccination campaign removes susceptibles at the start of its week.
