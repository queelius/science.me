# seir-core

This library is the standard susceptible-exposed-infectious-recovered model of a respiratory epidemic, stepped daily, with a mean latent period of 2 days and a mean infectious period of 3 days, typical of influenza. Transmission follows a yearly cosine (seasonal forcing, as in most influenza models), is scaled by `exp(-covariate_x * x)` for an environmental covariate, and is reduced by `school` times the share of schools closed, from the school calendar or a school-closure intervention. A vaccination intervention moves a fraction of susceptibles out of the epidemic at the start of its week. Weekly admissions are a fixed share of the week's new infections.

For each jurisdiction the transmission rate, seasonal amplitude and phase, initial susceptible and infectious shares, and the admission scale are fitted by least squares to the logarithm of the lens's nowcast, each week weighted by its precision under Poisson noise so that weeks of a few admissions do not outweigh weeks of hundreds. The mechanism strengths are held at the values a theory asserts. Holding them fixed rather than fitting them is what makes a theory a claim about a mechanism: two theories that differ only in one strength, each refitted on the same data, form a matched pair. The forecast carries the fit's own uncertainty: parameter sets drawn around the fit (its Laplace approximation, bounded by the plausible ranges) are each simulated to the target week, scaled to what the resolution vintage will report, and observed through the lens. Because a few months of data rarely settle when susceptibles will run out, the draws disagree most where the forecast reaches furthest past the data, and the forecast widens accordingly.

Known weakness: the forecasts are under-dispersed. On a synthetic epidemic generated by this very model with negative binomial noise, the pooled 90% intervals at horizons 0 to 3 weeks contain the realized count about 58 to 66% of the time, missing mostly on the high side. Sixteen noisy weeks cannot pin down six parameters, so the fit bends the noise into an early turnover, and the local approximation of its uncertainty is narrower than the spread of possible epidemics in that direction. The platform's standings are designed to surface exactly this kind of miscalibration, and a program that repairs it is a welcome successor.
